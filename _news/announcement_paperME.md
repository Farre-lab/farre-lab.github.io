New paper from the lab in Molecular Ecology!   

Corey's PhD work on Waterbuck chromosome evolution is just published - here we assembled a chromosome-level genome for the Defassa waterbuck, we sequenced 24 historical museum samples and combined it with modern samples to study population genetics and the impact of polymorphic Robertsonian fusions in this sepcies  

[Chromosome-level genomics and historical museum collections reveal new insights into population structure and chromosome evolution of waterbuck](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/mec.70218)
